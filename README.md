# CSI3210project

TopDeck Card Rater

Browse a variety of trading cards, from Magic and Pokémon to baseball and more, then rate your favorites and vote in community polls.

CSI 3210 Final Project, Fall 2026

Features
Browse and search cards by category
Rate cards from 1 to 5 stars and see the community average
Vote in community polls
Add cards that aren't in the database yet
Tech Stack
Backend: Python, Flask
Frontend: HTML, CSS, Jinja2 templates
Database: SQLite
Analytics: PostHog
